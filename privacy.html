一句話總結：本 App 的定位資料與行車記錄只在你的手機上處理， 我們沒有伺服器，也不會把你的位置、車速或行車軌跡傳送給我們。 唯一會接觸到外部的是 Google 地圖／街景與廣告服務（見第 4 節）。

1. 誰是營運者

本 App「香港交通相機預警」（套件名稱 com.nicfox.hktrafficcamera，下稱「本 App」） 由 NicFox Studio（下稱「我們」）開發及營運。

隱私相關查詢、資料刪除請求或投訴，請電郵 nicklam.ebiz@gmail.com，我們會在 30 日內回覆。

2. 本 App 做什麼

本 App 利用你手機的定位，計算你與香港運輸署已公布的測速執法相機、衝紅燈攝影機之間的距離， 在你接近時以粵語語音及懸浮窗提示，並顯示所在路段的法定限速。 本 App 只作提示，不會控制車輛，也不是官方執法工具。

3. 我們收集及使用的資料

3.1 精確位置（GPS）

項目內容
收集什麼裝置的精確位置（緯度、經度、海拔、速度、方向、定位精度）
為何收集計算與相機的距離、判斷你是否朝向該相機、顯示所在路段限速、超速提示
何時收集你開啟「背景提醒」開關期間；關閉後立即停止
如何處理全程在你的裝置上計算。不會上傳至我們或任何由我們營運的伺服器
可否拒絕可以。拒絕定位權限後，本 App 無法計算距離，因此核心功能不可用

3.2 行車記錄（本機檔案）

當「背景提醒」開啟時，本 App 會在你手機的應用程式專屬目錄寫入行車記錄：

Android/data/com.nicfox.hktrafficcamera/files/logs/

記錄內容包括：時間戳、位置座標、車速、航向、定位精度、觸發了哪些提示、播放了哪句語音。

  • 這些檔案只存在你的裝置上，我們無法讀取。
  • 用途是讓你自己核對「提示是否準確」，以及在你自己選擇的情況下匯出給第三方分析。
  • 匯出功能使用 Android 系統的分享面板，由你決定傳送去哪裡；一經你分享，該檔案的後續處理就受該目的地（例如通訊軟件、雲端硬碟）的政策規管。
  • 你可隨時在設定頁刪除，或解除安裝本 App 一併清除。

3.3 設定值

你調整的播報距離、同向容差、音色、懸浮窗透明度等，儲存在裝置本機的 SharedPreferences，不上傳。

3.4 我們不會收集的資料

  • 姓名、電郵、電話號碼、住址或任何帳戶資料（本 App 無需註冊）
  • 通訊錄、通話記錄、短訊
  • 相片、影片、麥克風錄音（本 App 不存取相機或麥克風）
  • 行事曆、健康資料、財務資料
  • 你裝置上的其他檔案

4. 第三方服務

本 App 使用以下 Google 服務。它們會直接從你的裝置接收資料，我們無法攔截或阻止。

4.1 Google Maps SDK for Android 及 Street View

用於顯示相機地圖及互動街景。Google 會接收地圖圖磚與街景的請求， 這些請求包含你目前顯示範圍的位置、IP 位址、裝置型號及作業系統版本。

相關政策： Google 隱私權政策 · Google 地圖附加服務條款

4.2 Google AdMob（廣告）

免費版本會顯示廣告。Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能收集並使用：

  • 廣告 ID（Android Advertising ID，可由你在系統設定重設或刪除）
  • IP 位址（用於粗略地區判斷及防止欺詐）
  • 裝置資料（型號、作業系統版本、語言、螢幕尺寸）
  • 廣告互動記錄（展示、點擊）
  • 應用程式使用情況（例如開啟次數），用於廣告頻率控制

個人化廣告只會在取得你的同意後提供。位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士的用戶 會先看到由 Google 認證的同意管理平台（UMP）發出的同意表格；拒絕個人化廣告不影響你使用本 App， 你仍會看到非個人化廣告。

你可隨時在 Android 系統設定 → Google → 廣告 中重設或刪除廣告 ID， 或選擇「不允許個人化廣告」。

付費移除廣告後，本 App 不會再載入廣告，亦不會初始化廣告 SDK 的請求。

相關政策： Google 廣告政策 · AdMob 政策

4.3 相機資料更新（由你主動觸發）

設定頁有一個「檢查線上更新」功能。只有你按下該按鈕時，本 App 才會以 GET 方式向你填寫的網址下載一份新的相機資料（JSON）。 該請求不包含你的位置或任何個人資料，只包含標準的 HTTP 標頭。

5. 我們會否出售或分享你的資料

不會。我們沒有你的資料可以出售 —— 位置與行車記錄從未離開你的裝置。 除第 4 節所述的 Google 服務外，我們不會向任何第三方披露、出售或轉移你的資料。

唯一例外是法律要求：如收到有效的法律程序（例如法院命令），而我們持有相關資料， 我們會依法配合。但如上所述，我們實際上並不持有你的位置或行車記錄。

Privacy Policy

HK Traffic Camera Alert · 香港交通相機預警

In one sentence: all location processing and driving logs stay on your device. We operate no server and never receive your location, speed, or driving history. The only external parties involved are Google's map, street-view and advertising services (see section 4).

1. Who we are

This app, HK Traffic Camera Alert (package com.nicfox.hktrafficcamera, the “App”), is developed and operated by NicFox Studio (“we”, “us”).

For privacy questions, data-deletion requests or complaints, email nicklam.ebiz@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

2. What the App does

The App uses your device's location to compute your distance to speed-enforcement cameras and red-light cameras published by the Hong Kong Transport Department. As you approach, it plays a Cantonese voice alert and shows a floating overlay, together with the posted speed limit for the road segment you are on. The App is advisory only. It does not control any vehicle and is not an official enforcement tool.

3. Data we collect and use

3.1 Precise location (GPS)

ItemDetail
CollectedPrecise location (latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, bearing, accuracy)
PurposeDistance to cameras, whether you are travelling toward them, speed limit of the current segment, overspeed warnings

RefusalYou may refuse. Without location the App cannot compute distances, so the core feature is unavailable

3.2 Driving logs (local files only)

While alerts are running, the App writes driving logs to its own app-specific directory on your device: Android/data/com.nicfox.hktrafficcamera/files/logs/

A log entry contains: timestamp, coordinates, speed, bearing, location accuracy, which alerts fired, and which voice clip played.

  • These files exist only on your device. We cannot read them.
  • Their purpose is for you to verify alert accuracy, and — if you choose — to export them for your own analysis.
  • Export uses the Android system share sheet. You decide the destination; once shared, that copy is governed by the destination's own policy (messaging app, cloud drive, …).
  • You can delete them at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

3.3 Settings

Alert distances, direction tolerance, voice choice, overlay opacity and similar preferences are stored in on-device SharedPreferences. Not uploaded.

3.4 What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, postal address, or any account data (the App requires no sign-up)
  • Contacts, call logs, SMS
  • Photos, videos, or microphone audio (the App does not access camera or microphone)
  • Calendar, health, or financial data
  • Other files on your device

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following Google services. They receive data directly from your device; we cannot intercept or prevent that.

4.1 Google Maps SDK for Android and Street View

Used to display the camera map and interactive street view. Google receives map-tile and panorama requests, which include the location of the area being displayed, your IP address, device model and OS version.

Policies: Google Privacy Policy · Google Maps Additional Terms of Service

4.2 Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version shows ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID — you can reset or delete it in system settings)
  • IP address (coarse region and fraud prevention)
  • Device data (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks)
  • App usage signals (e.g. open counts) for frequency capping

Personalised advertising is served only with your consent. Users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland are shown a consent form from Google's certified consent management platform (UMP) first. Declining personalised ads does not affect your use of the App — you will still see non-personalised ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

After purchasing ad removal, the App stops loading ads and does not issue ad SDK requests.

Policies: Google Advertising Policies · AdMob Policies

4.3 Camera-data update (initiated by you)

Settings offers a “Check for updates” action. Only when you tap it does the App issue a GET request to the URL you supply, downloading a camera-data JSON file. That request contains no location or personal data — only standard HTTP headers.

5. Do we sell or share your data?

No. We have no data to sell — your location and driving logs never leave your device. Apart from the Google services listed in section 4, we do not disclose, sell or transfer your data to anyone.

The only exception would be a valid legal process (e.g. a court order). As explained above, we do not in fact hold your location or driving logs.

8. Children

The App is a tool for drivers, intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The App is not enrolled in Google Play's Families programme.

9. Security

  • Traffic to Google services uses HTTPS.
  • There are no accounts, so no passwords to leak.
  • Note that a rooted device, physical access by someone else, or your own export of logs moves the protection of that copy into your hands (device settings, chosen destination).
